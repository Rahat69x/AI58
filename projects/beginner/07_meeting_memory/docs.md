## Executive Summary
AI assistant that analyzes meeting transcripts to remember decisions, participants, and commitments.

---

## Architectural Capabilities
- Transcript intake (raw text / VTT / SRT)
- Concise meeting summary
- Decisions made log
- Action items & owners extraction
- Participant list & engagement detection
- Deadlines timeline
- Searchable archive of past meetings
- Follow-up email generator

---

## Technical Stack & Dependencies
- **Core Languages**: Python, FastAPI
- **Libraries**: NLP Parser, Transcript Chunking, Decision Extractor
- **Shared Platform Services**: AI58 Unified AI Engine, Data Engine, Security Layer
